=== What each policy withdrew ===
Four categories, and the distinction between the middle two is the whole
point. `poisoned` was wrong even at its own site: a clean win.
`misled` was about the same fault and gave the wrong answer for this
episode, so the memory really did mislead and withdrawing it is
defensible. `irrelevant` was about a DIFFERENT fault and never applied:
collateral, and still knowledge about its own fault that the store has
now lost. `true here` was right for this very episode: pure loss.

=== Decomposing success into coverage and guessing ===
Each arm's success is P(a correct memory arrived) x P(right | it did)
plus P(none arrived) x P(right | none). The second term is the model
guessing from six options, and it is why a stripped store still scores
above zero - so success does NOT track coverage one-for-one, and the
plain ratio of the two is misleading for exactly the arms that strip the
store. The column that matters is `from memory`: the share of an arm's
success that a memory actually supplied.
